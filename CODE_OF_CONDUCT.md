# Cairn Community Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of Cairn, we are committed to fostering a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative open-source community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards of Behavior

We expect all participants in discussions, issues, and code reviews to:
- **Demonstrate empathy and respect**: Welcome different perspectives and treat everyone with courtesy.
- **Provide constructive feedback**: Focus on code quality, design principles, and problem-solving.
- **Collaborate transparently**: Share knowledge openly and acknowledge the contributions of others.
- **Prioritize safety and privacy**: Never post secrets, sensitive credentials, or private information in public issue trackers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated in any community space:
- Harassment, discrimination, or derogatory comments in any form.
- Trolling, insulting, or disruptive behavior.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Repeated disregard for project guidelines and team standards.

## Responsibilities & Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code reviews, issues, and other contributions that do not align with these guidelines.

To report violations, please reach out to the project maintainers via the repository issue tracker or designated contact channels.
